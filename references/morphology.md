# Turkmen morphology for editors

Suffixes, cases, verb forms, numbers and function words. Summarized in our own words from the morphology pages of enedilim.com; points marked *(usage)* come from general knowledge of Turkmen and standard press usage, not from those pages.

Contents: 1 suffix order · 2 sound changes at suffixes · 3 plural · 4 possessive · 5 cases · 6 pronouns · 7 "to be", negation, questions · 8 numbers and dates · 9 adjectives and comparison · 10 verbs · 11 postpositions · 12 conjunctions and particles · 13 adverbs and paired words · 14 Turkish → Turkmen table

## 1. Suffix order

root → word-forming suffix → plural → possessive → case → question or predicative suffix

`okuw + çy + lar + dan`, `ýurt + lar + ymyz + yň`

Suffixes attach directly, without space, hyphen or apostrophe. A hyphen appears only after digits and abbreviations (`2026-njy`, `BMG-niň`) and before particles (`bolsa-da`).

## 2. Sound changes at suffix boundaries

| Change | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| final `k p t ç` → `g b d j` | before a vowel suffix, in words of 2+ syllables and long-vowel one-syllable words | `kitap → kitaby`, `agaç → agajyň`, `balyk → balygy`, `ýürek → ýüregi`, `maksat → maksady` |
| no change | short-vowel one-syllable words; `-at/-et` loans | `saç → saçy`, `at` (horse) `→ aty`; `hökümeti`, `syýasaty` |
| second-syllable narrow vowel drops | 2-syllable nouns in `z l n r s ş` | `ogul → ogly`, `deňiz → deňzi`, `nagyş → nagşy` |
| final `-e` → `-ä` | before 1st/2nd person possessive and in genitive, dative, accusative; in verbs before `-p`, `-an`, `-ar` | `penjire → penjiräm`, `käse → käsäniň, käsä, käsäni`, `işle → işläp, işlän, işlär`, `Türkiýe → Türkiýäniň` |
| (exception) | kinship words | `ene`, `eje` keep `e` |
| final `-y/-i` → `-a/-ä` in the dative | | `gapy → gapa`, `Mary → Mara` |
| `t`, `k` → `d`, `g` before `-ýar`, `-ar` | some verbs | `aýt → aýdýar`, `git → gidýär`, `et → edýär`, `okat → okadýar` |

## 3. Plural `-lar / -ler`

- Back stem `-lar`, front stem `-ler`: `ýurtlar`, `welaýatlar`, `şäherler`, `işler`, `kompýuterler`.
- After a rounded first syllable a final `-y/-i` rounds: `guzy → guzular`, `goşgy → goşgular`.
- After a number the noun stays singular: `üç gün`, `bäş ýyl`, `10 müň adam`. Not `üç günler`.
- `ol → olar`, `şol → şolar`.

## 4. Possessive suffixes

| | After a consonant (back / front) | Rounded one-syllable stems | After a vowel |
|---|---|---|---|
| my | `-ym / -im` | `-um / -üm` | `-m` |
| your (sg) | `-yň / -iň` | `-uň / -üň` | `-ň` |
| his, her, their | `-y / -i` | | `-sy / -si` |
| our | `-ymyz / -imiz` | `-umyz / -ümiz` | `-myz / -miz` |
| your (pl, polite) | `-yňyz / -iňiz` | `-uňyz / -üňiz` | `-ňyz / -ňiz` |

- Rounded forms only with one-syllable stems containing `o ö u ü`: `sözüm`, `ýurdumyz`. Longer stems use the plain forms: `kitabymyz`, `depderim`.
- The 3rd person is the same for "his" and "their": `kitaby`; with a plural stem: `kitaplary`.
- Genitive construction: owner in the genitive, owned noun with the 3rd person possessive: `Türkmenistanyň Prezidenti`, `ýurdumyzyň ykdysadyýeti`. Place names with a generic word skip the genitive: `Aşgabat şäheri`, `Mary welaýaty`, `Balkan welaýatynda`.

## 5. Cases

### 5.1 Plain nouns

| Case | Question | After consonant | After vowel | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | kim? näme? | — | — | `bag`, `oba` |
| Genitive | kimiň? nämäniň? | `-yň / -iň` (`-uň / -üň`) | `-nyň / -niň` | `bagyň`, `obanyň`, `gölüň` |
| Dative | kime? nirä? | `-a / -e` | vowel lengthens or changes (`-a / -ä`) | `baga`, `şähere`, `oba`, `gapa`, `Mara` |
| Accusative | kimi? nämäni? | `-y / -i` | `-ny / -ni` | `bagy`, `güli`, `obany` |
| Locative | nirede? | `-da / -de` | `-da / -de` | `bagda`, `obada`, `mekdepde` |
| Ablative | nireden? | `-dan / -den` | `-dan / -den` | `bagdan`, `işden` |

- Locative and ablative always start with `d`: `Aşgabatda`, `işden`. Never `-ta/-tan` as in Turkish.
- The dative after a vowel never inserts `ý`: `oba`, `Ýewropa`, not `obaýa`.
- Final `-e` → `-ä` only in genitive, dative, accusative: `käsä, käsäni, käsäniň` but `käsede, käseden`; `Türkiýä`, `Türkiýäniň`, `Türkiýede`.

### 5.2 After possessive suffixes

After the 3rd person possessive an `n` appears:

| Case | my | his/their | our |
|---|---|---|---|
| Genitive | kitabymyň | **kitabynyň** | kitabymyzyň |
| Dative | kitabyma | **kitabyna** | kitabymyza |
| Accusative | kitabymy | **kitabyny** | kitabymyzy |
| Locative | kitabymda | **kitabynda** | kitabymyzda |
| Ablative | kitabymdan | **kitabyndan** | kitabymyzdan |

Common in news: `welaýatynda`, `şäherinde`, `ýylynda`, `obasyna`, `Prezidentiniň`, `kararyny`, `meýilnamasyny`, `wekilleriniň`, `işgärlerine`.

## 6. Pronouns

| | Nom. | Gen. | Dat. | Acc. | Loc. | Abl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | men | meniň | **maňa** | meni | mende | menden |
| you | sen | seniň | **saňa** | seni | sende | senden |
| he/she | ol | onuň | oňa | ony | onda | ondan |
| we | biz | biziň | bize | bizi | bizde | bizden |
| you (pl) | siz | siziň | size | sizi | sizde | sizden |
| they | olar | olaryň | olara | olary | olarda | olardan |
| this | bu | **munuň** | **muňa** | **muny** | munda | mundan |
| this | şu | şunuň | şuňa | şuny | şunda | şundan |
| that | şol | şonuň | şoňa | şony | şonda | şondan |

- `bu` becomes `mu-` before case endings. Turkish `bunu`, `onun`, `benim` are wrong in Turkmen.
- Reflexive `öz`: `özüm`, `özüň`, `özi`, `özümiz`, `özüne`; `öz ýurdumyz`.
- Separate words: `hiç kim`, `hiç zat`, `hiç wagt`, `her bir`, `her ýyl`, `her hili`. One word: `birnäçe`, `käbir`.
- `hemme`, `birnäçe` take `-ä-` before 1st/2nd person: `hemmämiz`, `birnäçämiz`.
- Question words: `kim`, `näme`, `haýsy`, `nähili`, `näçe`, `nirede`, `nirä`, `haçan`, `näme üçin` (two words).

## 7. "To be", negation, questions

- `-dyr / -dir` (`-dur / -dür` after rounded one-syllable stems): "is", or probability after a participle: `möhümdir`, `gelendir`. In the 3rd person it is often left out: `Bu möhüm wezipe.` / `Bu möhüm wezipedir.`
- `-dy / -di` + person: "was": `zordum`, `öýdedi`.
- `-myş / -miş`: hearsay: `işçimiş`. `eken` (separate word): "it turns out": `kyn eken`.
- `däl` is a separate word: `okuwçy däl`, `däldir`; `däl-de` with a hyphen for "not X but Y".
- `bar` / `ýok` for existence. Not `var`, `yok`, `değil`.
- Question suffix `-my / -mi` is written **together** with the word: `geldimi?`, `barmy?`, `dälmi?`, `Bu kitapmy?`. Not `geldi mi` as in Turkish. Present tense allows both `bilýärsiňmi` and `bilýärmisiň`.

## 8. Numbers and dates

- Compound numbers are separate words: `on iki`, `ýigrimi bäş`, `iki müň ýigrimi alty`.
- Ordinals in words: `birinji`, `ikinji`, `üçünji`, `dördünji`, `bäşinji`, `altynjy`, `ýedinji`, `sekizinji`, `dokuzynjy`, `onunjy`.
- Ordinals in digits: digit + hyphen + `-njy` or `-nji`, by the vowel of the last word as spoken:
  - `-njy`: `6-njy` (alty), `9-njy` (dokuz), `10-njy` (on), `30-njy` (otuz), `2026-njy`
  - `-nji`: `1-nji`, `2-nji`, `3-nji`, `5-nji`, `20-nji`, `100-nji`, `2025-nji`
- Roman numerals take no suffix: `XXI asyr`.
- After a full stop or bracket in a list, no suffix: `1. Ataýew`.
- Approximate pairs: `iki-üç gün`, `dört-bäş adam`.
- Case suffixes on digits *(usage)*: hyphen, harmonized to the spoken number: `2030-njy ýyla çenli`, `2020-nji ýyldan bäri`, `100-den gowrak`, `10-a çenli`.
- Dates *(usage)*: `2026-njy ýylyň 25-nji oktýabrynda`, `25-nji oktýabrda`. Months: `ýanwar, fewral, mart, aprel, maý, iýun, iýul, awgust, sentýabr, oktýabr, noýabr, dekabr` (lowercase). Weekdays: `duşenbe, sişenbe, çarşenbe, penşenbe, anna, şenbe, ýekşenbe`.
- Percent: `göterim`: `5 göterim`. Fractions: `dörtden üç` (3/4).
- `onlarça`, `ýüzlerçe`, `müňlerçe`; adverbs `onlap`, `müňläp`.

## 9. Adjectives and comparison

- An adjective before a noun takes no suffix; the noun does: `täze binalarda`.
- Derivation: `-ly/-li` (`bilimli`), `-syz/-siz` (`suwsuz`, `Garaşsyz`), `-ky/-ki` and `-daky/-däki` (`häzirki`, `öňki`, `şu günki`, `ýurdumyzdaky`, `Aşgabatdaky`).
- From `-at/-ýat/-et` loans: `edebiýat → edebi`, `medeniýet → medeni`, `syýasat → syýasy`.
- Comparative `-rak/-räk`, attached: `köpräk`, `kiçiräk`, `oňadrak`. Compared item in the ablative: `senden kiçiräk`.
- Superlative `iň` (separate word): `iň uly`, `iň möhüm`. Not `en`.
- "More": `has` (separate): `has uly`; `has-da` with a hyphen: `has-da ösdürmek`. Not `daha`.
- Intensifiers: `örän`, `juda` (separate); doubled forms with a hyphen: `ap-ak`, `göm-gök`, `dos-dogry`.

## 10. Verbs

### 10.1 Infinitive, negation, compound verbs

- Infinitive `-mak / -mek`: `almak`, `geçirmek`.
- Negation `-ma / -me` right after the root (and after a voice suffix): `gelmedi`, `geçirilmedi`.
- Compound verbs are two words: `kabul etmek`, `çykyş etmek`, `gol çekmek`, `amala aşyrmak`. Their passive goes on the helper verb: `kabul edildi`, `gol çekildi`.

### 10.2 Voice

| Voice | Suffix | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Passive | `-yl/-il/-ul/-ül`; `-yn/-in` after `l`; `-l` or `-n` after a vowel | `açylmak`, `berilmek`, `alynmak`, `okalmak`, `bellenmek`, `tassyklanmak` |
| Reflexive | `-yn/-in/-n` | `geýinmek` |
| Reciprocal | `-yş/-iş/-ş` | `gürleşmek`, `alyşmak` |
| Causative | `-dyr/-dir`; `-t` after polysyllables in a, e, r; some irregular | `ýazdyrmak`, `okatmak`, `işletmek`, `geçirmek`, `çykarmak` |

The causative is `-dyr`, never `-tyr`: `ýazdyr-`, not `ýaztyr-`. Official prose sometimes doubles the passive (`bellenildi`, `tassyklanyldy`); follow house style.

### 10.3 Present tense `-ýar / -ýär`

| | Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| men | okaýaryn, bilýärin | okamaýaryn, bilmeýärin |
| sen | okaýarsyň | okamaýarsyň |
| ol | okaýar, bilýär | okamaýar, bilmeýär |
| biz | okaýarys, bilýäris | okamaýarys |
| siz | okaýarsyňyz | okamaýarsyňyz |
| olar | okaýarlar | okamaýarlar |

- Stems in `-a/-e` keep the vowel: `ýaşaýar`, `gözleýär` (not `ýaşyýar`).
- Not Turkish `-iyor`: `gelýär`, not `geliyor`.
- Progressive with helper verbs: `işläp otyr`, `ösüp barýar`.
- Colloquial negatives `alamok`, `göremok` belong in dialogue; news uses `-maýar/-meýär`.

### 10.4 Past tenses

| Tense | Form | Examples | Negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Definite past (news default) | `-dy/-di` + `-m, -ň, —, -k, -ňyz, -lar` | `aldym, aldyň, aldy, aldyk, aldyňyz, aldylar`, `geçirildi`, `bellendi` | `almady`, `gelmedi` |
| Reported / inferred past | `-ypdyr/-ipdir`, `-pdyr` after a vowel | `gelipdir`, `okapdyr`, `işläpdir` | `gelmändir` |
| Past perfect | `-ypdy/-ipdi` | `gelipdi`, `gelipdik` | `gelmändi` |
| Past continuous | `-ýardy/-ýärdi` | `gelýärdi`, `okaýardy` | `gelmeýärdi` |
| Habitual past | `-ardy/-erdi` | `okardy`, `gelerdi`, `işlärdi` | `gelmezdi` |

- The past suffix is always `-dy/-di`, never `-ty/-ti`: `aýtdy`, `gitdi`, `ýapdy`.
- Turkish `gelmiş` is `gelipdir` in Turkmen; Turkmen `-myş` is only hearsay.
- `-yp/-ip` + `-myş/-miş` is Turkmen, not Turkish: `tanalypmyş`, `gelipmiş` ("it turns out it became known", "reportedly came"). Keep it as the source has it; changing it to `-ypdyr` drops the hearsay. Only the bare Turkish form (`gelmiş`, `tanınmış`) is an error. Confirmed by a native speaker after a test that changed `tanalypmyş` to `tanalypdyr`.

### 10.5 Future

| Form | Use | Examples | Negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| `-jak/-jek` | planned, certain | `geljek`, `geçiriljek`, `Biz geljek` | `geljek däl` |
| `-ar/-er` | likely, habitual | `alar`, `berer`, `işlär`, `aýdar`, `gider`; `alaryn, alarsyň, alar, alarys, alarsyňyz, alarlar` | `almaz`, `bermez`, `almaryn`, `almarys` |
| `-makçy/-mekçi` | intention | `gitmekçi`, `gurmakçy` | `gitmekçi däl` |

### 10.6 Moods

| Mood | Forms | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Imperative, you (sg) | root | `ýaz`, `gör`, `gitme` |
| Imperative, you (pl, polite) | `-yň/-iň`, `-ň` after a vowel | `geliň`, `alyň`, `okaň` |
| I, let me | `-aýyn/-eýin` | `ýazaýyn`, `barlaýyn` |
| we, let's | `-aly/-eli`, `-alyň/-eliň` | `alaly`, `edeliň`, `geliň, seredeliň` |
| let him/them | `-syn/-sin`, `-synlar/-sinler` | `bolsun`, `gelsin`, `başlasynlar` |
| Conditional | `-sa/-se` + person | `alsam, alsaň, alsa, alsak`; `gelmese`; `eger gelse` |
| Concessive | `-sa-da/-se-de` | `gelse-de` |
| Necessity | `-maly/-meli` | `etmeli`, `berilmeli`; `etmeli däl` |
| Wish | `-sady/-sedi` | `gutarsadym` |

### 10.7 Participles and "that" clauses

| Participle | Affirmative | Negative | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Past | `-an/-en` (`-än` after `-e` stems) | `-madyk/-medik` | `geçirilen maslahat`, `işlän`, `tapylmadyk` |
| Present | `-ýan/-ýän` | `-maýan/-meýän` | `işleýän adam` |
| Future | `-jak/-jek`, `-ar/-er` | `-majak/-mejek`, `-maz/-mez` | `geçiriljek çäre`, `akar suw` |

Reported "that" clauses, very common in news *(usage)*: participle + `-dygy/-digi` + possessive + case, written as one word:

- `bolandygyny belledi` (noted that it was)
- `geçiriljekdigi habar berildi` (it was announced that it will be held)
- `ösýändigini görkezýär` (shows that it is growing)
- Turkish `geldiğini` = Turkmen `gelendigini`.

### 10.8 Converbs

| Form | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `-yp/-ip`, `-p` after a/e (`e → ä`) | and; by doing | `ýazyp`, `başlap`, `sözläp`, `çykyş edip` |
| `-yp bilmek` (two words) | can | `gelip biler`, `gelip bilmez`. Not `gelebilir` |
| `-man/-män` | without doing | `gelmän`, `alman` |
| `-anda/-ende` | when | `gelende`, `geçirilende` |
| `-andan soň` | after | `geleninden soň` |
| `-maga/-mäge`, `-mak üçin` | in order to | `ösdürmäge`, `ösdürmek üçin` |
| `-ýança/-ýänçä` | until | `gelýänçä` |
| `-aly bäri` | since | `geleli bäri` |
| `-yşy ýaly` | as | `bellenilişi ýaly` |
| `-magy/-megi` + possessive | verbal noun | `gurulmagy`, `ösdürilmegine` |

## 11. Postpositions

| `tarapyndan` | by (agent) | nominative | `hökümet tarapyndan` | tarafından |
| `çenli` | until, up to | dative | `2030-njy ýyla çenli` | kadar |
| `görä` | according to | dative | `karara görä` | göre |

## 12. Conjunctions and particles

- `we` (not `ve`), `hem`, `hem-de`, `emma`, `ýöne`, `ýa`, `ýa-da`, `ne ... ne-de`, `sebäbi`, `çünki` (not `çünkü`), `eger`, `ýagny`, `bolsa` ("as for": `Ol bolsa ...`), `şeýle hem`, `şeýle-de`.
- Hyphenated: `hem-de`, `ýa-da`, `ne-de`, `şeýle-de`, `has-da`, `däl-de`, `bolsa-da`, `geldi-de`.
- `hem` merged into the previous word (`menem`, `kitabam`) is colloquial; formal text writes `hem` separately.
- Particles with a hyphen: `-da/-de`, `-la/-le`, `-a/-ä`, `-ha/-hä`: `Ol-a geldi`, `gel-dä`.
- Separate-word particles: `diňe`, `hut`, `hatda`, `entek`, `eýýäm`.
- Surnames end in `-ow/-ew`, `-owa/-ewa` (`-ýew/-ýewa` after a vowel): `Myradow`, `Ataýewa`; suffixes attach directly: `Ataýewiň`.

## 13. Adverbs and paired words

- Attached: `türkmençe`, `rusça`, `iňlisçe`, `günsaýyn`, `ömürboýy`, `müňläp`, `irden`.
- Single-stress compounds as one word: `günuzyn`, `birnäçe`. Phrases as separate words: `şu gün`, `ilkinji gezek`.
- Hyphenated doubles and pairs: `ýyl-ýyldan`, `gün-günden`, `ýyldan-ýyla`, `günden-güne`, `wagtly-wagtynda`, `gije-gündiz`, `ýüzbe-ýüz`, `ýaňy-ýakynda`.
